How it works

  1. 01

    Upload your material

    Add a lecture PDF, presentation, Word document or revision handout. Text is extracted with page, slide and section references kept intact.

  2. 02

    Generate a study package

    Gemini analyses your material and produces an overview, topics, revision notes, a glossary, formulas, flashcards and practice questions.

  3. 03

    Practise and check yourself

    Sit a timed mock examination, review explanations for every answer, and ask the tutor to explain anything that is still unclear.

Built for focused revision

Everything is tied back to the material you uploaded, with source references you can check.

Your material, not generic notes

Summaries, definitions and questions are generated from your own documents. Citations point to the page, slide or section they came from.

Practice that feels like the real exam

Timed mock examinations with question navigation, automatic scoring for objective questions and an explanation for every answer.

A tutor that knows the source

Ask follow-up questions and the tutor answers from the relevant sections of your document, and says so when the material does not cover something.

Private by design

Your documents are stored outside the public web root, every request checks ownership, and you can delete a document together with everything generated from it.
