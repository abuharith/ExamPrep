Privacy

How ExamPrep handles your material

This page explains what happens to a document after you upload it.

What is stored

ExamPrep stores your account details, the original file in private storage, the text extracted from it, the study packages generated from that text, your practice answers, mock examination attempts and tutor conversation. Uploaded files are never served from a public URL.

How your material is processed

Text extracted from your document is sent to Google's Gemini API so that the study package and tutor answers can be generated. Only the extracted text needed for the current request is sent, together with your study preferences. Your password and your API configuration are never sent to Gemini.

What is not done

ExamPrep does not use your documents for advertising, does not share them with other students, and does not present invented material as if it came from your course. Generated content is educational assistance and should always be checked against the original material.

Deleting your material

Deleting a document removes its stored file, its extracted text and every study package generated from it, including flashcards, questions, attempts and tutor conversations. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Questions

If you are running your own copy of ExamPrep, the person who deployed it is responsible for the Gemini API account and for your institution's data policy.
